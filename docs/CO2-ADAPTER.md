# Build the Apollo 13 CO₂ adapter

Press **F12** after finishing the power-down to carry the cabin forward to
Kerwin's first adapter callout at **090:09:17 GET**. **F10** starts directly from
the reconstructed cold configuration when there is no power-down in progress.
**Shift+F10** starts over from that configuration. The several hours of coast
between chapters are condensed into a cold starting state. The handoff retains
the completed electrical configuration and battery state.

Press **F** to locate each part or control. Click the marked part, then click its
destination. When Kerwin calls for a valve position, use the actual ECS control.
**Shift+click** reverses a control. **F5 / F9** saves and restores the assemblies,
CO₂ estimate and shared simulation state. A loose part selection clears on load.
Press **R** to replay the latest Houston transmission, or **M** to mute or
unmute the radio. The action captions stay on the checklist.

The [flight transcript](https://www.apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/15day4-mailbox.html)
supplies the CAPCOM captions and action order. Use two square Odyssey LiOH
cartridges, gray tape, LCG inner bags, a stiff EVA cue card, scissors, a towel
scrap, and the LM suit hoses. The first red hose goes into the LMP bag during
assembly. The CDR hose is inserted later, after Houston calls back. Secure the
LMP cartridge by the EPS panel and the CDR cartridge by the tunnel. The LMP blue
outlet goes to the LMP window and the extended CDR outlet leads into the tunnel.

The artist's work surface and prop placements inside the cabin are reconstructed.
Picking up and using a tool represents a cut, fold, tape wrap or hose connection.
The chapter covers the first pair of adapters. The extra cartridges later stacked
onto those units are a subsequent procedure. [Mission Report §6.7 and Fig. 6.7-1](https://www.apollojournals.org/alsj/a13/A13_MissionReport.pdf)

The panel 2 reading and the caption show a scenario CO₂ estimate in mmHg. The
pressure falls when both adapters are installed, the suit-loop valves are set,
and the selected suit fan has power. Loss of the sensor supply blanks the
instrument. The warm-to-cold transition and CO₂ trend are compressed gameplay
models. Kerwin's **0.2 mmHg** report at **094:33:21 GET** was a ground reading.
The earlier cockpit gauge and Houston's reading sometimes disagreed.
[Post-installation transcript](https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/16day4-checkingodyssey.html)

## Historical radio

The first call plays when the chapter opens. Each new recorded instruction
plays when its checklist action becomes current. Several adjacent actions can
share one transmission, and the second cartridge uses the earlier build calls
where Kerwin asked the crew to repeat the procedure. The 094:33:21 report plays
once when the powered gauge first displays **0.2 mmHg** with the adapters
running. It stays available on **R** after a completed save is restored.
Press **F12** at the completed reading to continue into the
[manual course correction](MANUAL-COURSE-CORRECTION.md).
